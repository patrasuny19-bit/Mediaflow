# Privacy Policy — Starter

Replace this starter policy with one reviewed for your actual deployment.

MediaFlow should collect only information necessary to operate the service. Temporary media files should be deleted after their configured expiry. Reports submitted through `/api/report` should be stored only in a protected system with appropriate retention controls.

Before launch, document your actual:
- hosting providers
- analytics
- cookies
- logs
- storage
- retention period
- user rights/contact process
- third-party APIs
